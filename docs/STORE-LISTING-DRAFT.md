# Store listing draft

These are text drafts for review after device validation, not a submitted listing.

**App name:** Battle Monsters Mobile

**Subtitle / short description:** Your world. Their battleground.

**Description**

Some bonds are out of this world. Yours starts here.

Meet ten extraordinary creatures, each with its own story, personality, elemental strengths, and signature move. From Pyrax, the Ember Sovereign, to Mycelis, the Little Wonder, your next companion is waiting in the living bestiary.

Choose your monster, enter the solo arena, and learn the rhythm of elemental combat. Build energy, time your signature ability, and turn a close battle into a victory worth sharing. Earn essence, complete quests, and grow your summoner bond.

Bring your grimoire into your world with a holographic camera overlay. Show an open palm to reveal your book, then tap a name to summon. Supported devices can also recognize spoken monster names.

Chat with built-in monster personalities, available without an AI subscription. Optional AI conversations require a configured online service and are subject to that service’s availability.

**Features represented in this build**

- Ten animated 3D monsters with unique stats, elements, stories, and abilities.
- Solo turn-based battles and elemental strategy.
- A camera grimoire with on-device hand tracking and manual preview.
- Voice summoning where the device supports speech recognition.
- Companion conversations, shared bond progression, and local saves.
- No ads or purchases in this development version.

The game uses actual 3D meshes and WebGL rendering, with camera compositing driven by hand landmarks. Do not market it as depth-anchored spatial AR, headset VR, multiplayer, or cloud-synced. Only advertise optional live AI after the production authentication and provider integration is complete.
